# Intercontinental Exchange (ICE)

> Intercontinental Exchange (ICE) operates global exchanges, clearing houses, and data services for financial and commodity markets, including the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE). Developers access ICE market data through the Developer Center at developer.theice.com (Consolidated Feed API), the IDS Portal for real-time data integration, and the ICE Mortgage Technology Developer Portal for mortgage lending applications.

Generated by API Evangelist from the intercontinental-exchange catalog repo (no provider-published llms.txt was found on any ICE host).

## APIs

- [ICE Consolidated Feed API](https://developer.theice.com/hc/en-us): Real-time and delayed consolidated market data from exchanges operated by ICE including ICE Futures and NYSE. Base URL https://api.theice.com
- [ICE Data Services API](https://idsportal.icedataservices.com/marketdata): Market data, reference data, and analytics via the IDS Portal.
- [ICE Mortgage Technology Developer Portal](https://mortgagetech.ice.com/products/developer-portal): Self-service resources for building mortgage lending applications on the Encompass platform.
